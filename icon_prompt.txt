myGeeKy icon — AI image generation prompt
==========================================

Use this prompt with an image generation tool (DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable
Diffusion, etc.) to produce the project logo. Suggested output size: 512x512
or 1024x1024, exported as a transparent PNG, then cropped to a circle for
PyPI/GitHub avatar use.

Prompt:
--------
A cute, simple, flat-design circular icon logo. Inside the circle, two
cartoon "geek" characters (round glasses, friendly smiling faces, one with
messy hair and a laptop-print hoodie, the other with a bowtie and a
"</>"-print t-shirt) are hugging each other warmly, viewed from the front.
Around them, a few small minimalist red heart icons float in the air like
confetti, evenly spaced near the top edge of the circle. Soft pastel flat
color palette (light blue/teal background circle, warm skin tones, one
character in orange, one in purple), thick clean vector outlines, no
gradients, no text, no shadows, centered composition, generous padding
inside the circle, app-icon / mascot-logo style, minimalist, friendly,
inclusive, vector illustration, white background outside the circle.

Negative prompt (avoid):
--------
text, letters, watermark, photorealistic, 3D render, complex background,
extra limbs, distorted faces, clutter, gradients, drop shadows.


Prompt for Gemini (Nano Banana / Imagen)
==========================================
Gemini's image generation doesn't use a separate negative-prompt field, so
everything to avoid is folded into one natural-language description below.
Paste this as-is.

Prompt:
--------
Design a simple, flat, vector-style app icon, circular badge format, for a
friendly open-source tool called "myGeeKy." Inside the circle: two goofy,
excited cartoon "geek" characters mid-hug, viewed from the front, arms
wrapped tightly around each other, big joyful grins, eyes closed with
happiness. Give them fun, slightly wild, "crazy scientist" energy — messy
tousled hair sticking up, oversized round glasses slightly crooked, one
wearing a hoodie printed with a laptop icon, the other wearing a t-shirt
printed with a "</>" code symbol, maybe one of them mid-jump with excitement
so their feet lift off the ground. Centered directly above or around the
two characters, a single bold, clean, solid red heart shape (or a small
cluster of 3-4 small hearts floating like confetti), symbolizing warmth and
connection. Use a soft, friendly pastel color palette: a light teal or
sky-blue circular background, warm skin tones, one character's outfit in
orange, the other's in purple, thick clean black or dark-navy vector
outlines, completely flat shading with no gradients and no drop shadows.
Centered composition with generous padding inside the circle so nothing
touches the edge. No text or letters anywhere in the image. Clean white
(or transparent) background outside the circle. The overall feel should be
minimalist, playful, warm, and inclusive — a mascot logo suitable for a
small square app icon or a round PyPI/GitHub avatar, similar in spirit to
a modern flat-design emoji or sticker pack illustration, not photorealistic
and not 3D-rendered.
