Research NEW

The papers behind the code, and the people behind the papers

From OpenAlex, matched to your own research topics.

  • Papers in your field gaining citations fastest, measured in citations per month
  • New tools whose code is on GitHub. Click a paper to see its repo and the people working on it
  • A green P on any repo that has a published paper
News NEW

What's new in your field, not everyone's

Fresh items from arXiv, bioRxiv and Hacker News, kept only when they genuinely match your interests: two of your terms, or one exact phrase.

  • Each item says why it's there ("matches gwas, fine mapping")
  • 🔭 items from outside your bubble are marked, on purpose
How it learns

See what myGeeKy knows about you

The Model tab draws it as a living map: you in the middle, your research field around you, what you've been into lately pulsing further out, and today's new territory on the edge.

  • Clicks teach it a little, stars and forks more, follows the most
  • Interests fade with a 30-day half-life, so it follows you as you change
  • A separate model learns who follows you back

Keywords NEW

Keywords that mean something

Add "AI" and myGeeKy also looks for LLMs, deep learning and AI agents. Add "GWAS" and it brings in genomics and statistical genetics. A small model learns what goes together from GitHub's open data.

● Green: it knows

Keywords in the dictionary bring their whole meaning to People, Repos, Research and News, each related idea weighted by how closely it goes with yours.

● Red: not yet

Your own words still work, as plain words. They're passed on, through your beacon or with one click, so the next dictionary learns them.

🔁 It keeps learning

A new dictionary reaches everyone by itself, at most once a week. No server, nothing hidden: the model is a few hundred lines you can read.

Signals

Gratitude, not small talk

🙏📚⭐👀🤝

A quiet way to tell someone their work mattered to you: no server, no text, nothing to moderate, and nothing to answer.

  • Private: each signal is encrypted so only its recipient can read it. Nobody can see who you signalled, or who didn't answer.
  • No reply needed: every signal says so, and you never see "unanswered"
  • 🤝 only if you both want it: "let's collaborate" stays hidden until it's mutual
  • Courteous by design: once a month per person, a few new people a week, a "not taking signals" switch, mute and block

Privacy & trust

Suggest-only, by design

There is no follow, star or fork code in myGeeKy at all. And it uses two GitHub tokens with very different powers. Setup checks what each can actually do, and refuses one that doesn't fit its job.

🔍 Token 1 · read-only

Reads public profiles, repos and followers.
Repositories
Public repositories
Permissions
Followers: Read-only
Refused if
it can write to any repo, can follow people, or is your Signals token

✍️ Token 2 · Signals (optional)

Writes your mygeeky-beacon repo, and nothing else.
Repositories
Only mygeeky-beacon
Permissions
Contents: Read and write
Refused if
it can't write to the beacon, or is token 1 again

🔑 Keyring only

Tokens live in your OS keyring (Credential Locker, Keychain, Secret Service), never in a file, never logged.

🏠 Your data stays yours

No server, no account, no telemetry. Sync, if you want it, goes to a private GitHub repo you own.

📖 Open source

MIT-licensed and on GitHub, with tests. Read exactly what it does, and change it if you like.

Get started

Up and running in two minutes

Install

On Windows, run the installer: Next → Next → Finish. It sets up Python and Git if you don't have them. Anywhere else:

pip install mygeeky

Tell it who you are

Your GitHub username, plus your CV, ORCID or Scholar profile if you like, and token 1.

mygeeky init

Open the panel

Everything, live. The first run sets up the GUI by itself.

mygeeky gui
💡

Got an idea? A problem?

Click 💡 in the panel. Your browser opens a ready-to-send issue in myGeeKy-ideas, and you press Submit. Nothing is sent until you do.
